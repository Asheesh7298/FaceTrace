The idea

One face in. A verified identity out.

FaceTrace identifies a person from a single photo, finds their genuine social presence, and creates cryptographic proof of the finding that can never be silently changed.

face→ identify→ verify→ find profile→ anchor on-chain
How it works

Three simple stages

🧠
Stage 1
Detect & encode
MTCNN finds the face, creates a SHA-256 fingerprint and face embeddings.
🔎
Stage 2
Identify & search
Google Lens + AWS + a local index name the person — then find their real profile.
⛓️
Stage 3
Blockchain proof
Hash the finding, anchor it on-chain, then re-verify and tamper-test it.
Why it's accurate

🛡️ It never shows the wrong person

Before claiming any name, FaceTrace downloads real reference photos and re-matches them against the input. It only claims an identity when the faces truly match.

✅ Faces match

The identity is confirmed and shown, with the real profile link.

✕ No match

Shown honestly as "not confirmed" — never a fake or wrong result.

Not hardcoded

A real search — every time

Nothing is pre-mapped. Every identity is computed live by multiple independent engines running in parallel, exactly like a commercial face-search engine.

Google Lens AWS Rekognition 17,948-face index web profile search

More engines agreeing = higher confidence.

The proof

Tamper-evident on the blockchain

📜
Anchor
A SHA-256 of the finding is stored in the FaceProof contract.
🔁
Re-verify
Read it back — the on-chain hash still matches. Proof it's unchanged.
🧪
Tamper test
Change one field → the hash vanishes from the ledger. Tampering is caught.

Runs on Hardhat locally and on the public Ethereum Sepolia testnet.
